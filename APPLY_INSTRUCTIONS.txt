-------------------------------------------------------------------

- Every page now carries an Organization schema block (name, logo,
  Instagram, description) via src/app/layout.tsx.
- /locations now carries a CafeOrCoffeeShop block for all 7 open outlets.
- Each new city page (below) carries CafeOrCoffeeShop blocks for just that
  city's open outlets.

  the real hours you confirmed from Google Business Profile on 2026-09-15.
- Coming Soon outlets (Civil Line, Slice By 1DM VIP Road, Jagdalpur) are
  deliberately left OUT of the schema — marking a cafe that isn't open yet
  as an operating LocalBusiness would be inaccurate, not just incomplete.

The caveat — addresses: I only have real, confirmed street-level address
info for 2 of your 7 open outlets, both pulled from screenshots you already
sent me (the "Hours" tab of each GMB listing happened to show a sliver of
the address panel behind the dialog):
  - Dumartarai: "…Corporate Towers, NH-30, New Raipur, Chhattisgarh 492015"
    (building name still cut off in the screenshot)

    cut off)

Apply it
--------
cd ~/Downloads
rm -rf 1dm-seo-schema
ZIP=$(ls -t 1dm-seo-schema*.zip | head -1)
unzip -o "$ZIP" -d 1dm-seo-schema

cd /home/coffee/Downloads/1dm-website

rm -rf .next

npm run start

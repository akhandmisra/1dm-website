1DM — llms.txt for AI/SEO discovery
====================================

What this is
-------------
llms.txt is an emerging standard (llmstxt.org) — a plain-text/markdown file
at the root of a site that AI crawlers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google's
AI Overviews, etc.) can read to get accurate, structured facts about a
business instead of having to parse full HTML pages. It doesn't replace
normal SEO, but it makes it much more likely AI answers about 1DM are
correct and current rather than guessed or outdated.

What's in it
-------------
public/llms.txt covers, in plain language:
- What 1DM is, and how it relates to Kruptos Coffee Roasters and
  kruptoscoffee.com (so an AI doesn't try to send someone to 1dm.coffee to
  buy beans)
- All 10 locations, their real hours (from the GMB round), and which 3 are
  still Coming Soon
- Menu categories and the Bilaspur bakery partnership
- Kruptos's current drop
- 1DM Elite Club pricing and perks
- The 5 bar roasters
- Founder + team credentials
- Brand milestones/timeline
- Links to every key page

I pulled all of this from the same site-data.ts facts already on the site
(plus the contact email and Instagram handle already in the footer/contact
page) — nothing here is invented, and it matches what's live once you've
pushed the outlet-name and hours updates from the last two rounds.

Where it lives
---------------
- public/llms.txt — this is the important part. Since it's in the `public`
  folder, Next.js serves it directly at https://1dm.coffee/llms.txt with no
  routing needed — that's the actual convention AI crawlers look for (like
  robots.txt or sitemap.xml, it needs to be at the site root, not buried in
  a page).
- src/components/footer.tsx — added a small "llms.txt" text link next to
  the city list at the very bottom of the footer, since you asked for it to
  be reachable from the footer too. It's a plain text link, doesn't disturb
  the existing footer layout/columns.

Nothing else needed to change — this is a 2-file patch.

Apply it
--------
cd ~/Downloads
rm -rf 1dm-llms-txt
ZIP=$(ls -t 1dm-llms-txt*.zip | head -1)
unzip -o "$ZIP" -d 1dm-llms-txt

cd /home/coffee/Downloads/1dm-website
cp -r ~/Downloads/1dm-llms-txt/. /home/coffee/Downloads/1dm-website/

git add -A
git commit -m "Add llms.txt for AI/SEO discovery, link it in the footer"
git push

-------------

  20/20 pages. Also started the built site locally and confirmed
